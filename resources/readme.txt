Duke Nukem 3D (Shareware)
=========================

"L.A. Meltdown", the shareware episode of Duke Nukem 3D (3D Realms, 1996), played through JFDuke3D, Jonathon
Fowler's port of the original source. The v1.3d shareware data is included. To play the full game, put your
own duke3d.grp (the registered version) in this folder in place of the shareware one.


Controls
--------

D-Pad / left stick   Move forward and back, turn
Right stick          Strafe, look up and down (on a pad that has one)
Cross                Fire
Circle               Crouch
Triangle             Jump
Square               Open, activate (double press: kick)
L1                   Next inventory item (double press: use it)
R1                   Next weapon
L2 / R2              Strafe left / right
Select               Map (double press: toggle autorun)
Start                Menu

To leave the game: press Reset on the console, or hold Start + Select (the console, the Pi and the PC
stick). On Windows: Start for the menu, then Quit.

The settings (duke3d.cfg) and the saved games are kept in this folder.
